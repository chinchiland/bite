# Calorie apps on YouTube, 2025 to 2026: what Bite takes

Fourteen YouTube reviews and tests, their transcripts read in full (October 2026). Every source links to the moment in the video. Sponsorships are noted per video.

**In short**

- AI photo apps are judged on how fast you can fix the estimate. Bite already edits each ingredient in grams and counts hidden fat; next, the note that names the biggest doubt becomes a one-tap question (#1).
- The most praised feature in the category is MacroFactor's target that learns from your weight trend. Bite's "If you keep this up" forecast is one step from it (#2, #3).
- People quit over paywalled basics, nags, long setup, streaks that punish and AI results they can't edit. Bite avoids most of these; its streak was the exception (#4).
- Shipped on 12 October 2026: the doubt as a one-tap question (#1, without the range yet), a goal that learns from your weighings (#2, offered on the forecast card, not yet weekly), trend weight (#3), the forgiving streak (#4), your usual meal (#6), the photo's own time (#7), export (#14), and a search that forgives a typo (punished #13).

## What reviewers punish

1. Paywalled basics and nagging: MyFitnessPal's barcode scanner went behind the paywall ([V8 03:08](https://youtu.be/y9Hz24tgQew?t=188)); FatSecret is praised for never nagging.
2. Ads ([V3 03:45](https://youtu.be/8jao4tmlL1k?t=225)).
3. Long setup and chatty notifications ([V8 11:17](https://youtu.be/y9Hz24tgQew?t=677), [V11 07:25](https://youtu.be/V_R8zUnYNPY?t=445)).
4. AI results you can't edit item by item ([V11 08:32](https://youtu.be/V_R8zUnYNPY?t=512), [V7 09:48](https://youtu.be/pWCfRVtgols?t=588)).
5. Different answers for the same plate; a camera tip helps (foods separated, a bowl tipped onto a plate) ([V2 03:32](https://youtu.be/-_Ds_xDpGN4?t=212)).
6. One food, many conflicting entries ([V12 07:51](https://youtu.be/smbfaQdewDs?t=471)). Bite's one leading number per dish answers it.
7. A US-only food world ([V8 07:27](https://youtu.be/y9Hz24tgQew?t=447)). Swiss staples, Migros and Coop matter more than breadth.
8. Targets too low, or macros that don't follow the calories ([V13 06:07](https://youtu.be/kX8xrzSVJ4M?t=367)).
9. No sanity check on a weigh-in ([V10 04:42](https://youtu.be/z-Ex1uSpXcc?t=282)).
10. Basics hidden in settings ([V10 07:36](https://youtu.be/z-Ex1uSpXcc?t=456)).
11. Redesign churn ([V8 02:19](https://youtu.be/y9Hz24tgQew?t=139)).
12. Grades that feel arbitrary or judgmental ([V3 06:25](https://youtu.be/8jao4tmlL1k?t=385)).
13. Literal search: a typo finds nothing ([V8 17:34](https://youtu.be/y9Hz24tgQew?t=1054)).
14. Overclaiming accuracy ([V1 00:25](https://youtu.be/YgYeRZqiY5Y?t=25)). Say "estimate" and show the doubt.
15. Making money from the log ([V12 06:16](https://youtu.be/smbfaQdewDs?t=376)). Bite doesn't, and should say so.
